01

Overview

Purpose: Position agritourism as a district economic diversification tool, not as a resort scheme. Key local indicators used: Kurnool district area 7,977 sq km, population 22,71,686, 519 villages. Orvakallu Rock Garden is an existing tourism anchor near Kurnool.

Market signal: large domestic tourism base, limited farm experience supply

Domestic tourists are the core market; schools, families and weekend travellers are priority segments.

Where Kurnool fits

Weekend + school demand

Accessible, day-trip friendly, education-led farm visits

Horticulture diversity

Mango, banana, sweet orange, pomegranate, guava; onion & chillies

Experience economy

Urban families seek authentic rural food, open space and hands-on learning

Farmer resilience

Supplementary income reduces dependence on volatile crop prices and rainfall

Educational Tour

Demonstration of natural farming and other agri practices

Core proposition for the district

Convert existing farms into curated learning destinations with low additional capital.

India recorded 2,509 million domestic tourist visits during 2023; this establishes the scale of the addressable domestic tourism market. Kurnool horticulture offers farm-content depth: fruit, vegetable, spice and flower production.

Case study anchor: ATDC Baramati, Maharashtra

The model demonstrates how working farms can add tourism income without replacing agriculture.

02

PILOT LAUNCHED

2005

ATDC was incorporated with a 38-acre pilot near Pune / Baramati and trained farmers to run agritourism as supplementary income.

Farmer-owned assets

Use land, crops, livestock, food and culture already present.

ATDC support

Training, project reports, marketing and tour coordination.

Urban visitors

Schools, families, corporates and tourists from nearby cities.

Local income

Fees, meals, stays, farm produce and employment.

Transferable lesson

ATDC works because it centralises skills and market access, while farmers retain ownership and authenticity.

ATDC/Agri Tourism India is a facilitator and market-making organisation. The important design principle for Kurnool is not to build resorts first; it is to package working farms with training, hygiene, safety and marketing.

How the ATDC model operates

A hub-and-spoke system: central support + farmer-owned farm experiences.

Identify farms

Land + farmer interest + accessibility

Train & certify

Hospitality, hygiene, safety, pricing

Package visits

Food, farm walk, rides, culture, stays

Market centrally

Schools, families, corporates, tour operators

Deliver locally

Farmer, SHGs, youth, artisans host visitors

Institutional spine

Agriculture + Tourism departments / district administration provide registration, convergence, quality checks, school-linkages and risk governance.

Farmer remains the entrepreneur

District platform reduces marketing cost

This slide explains the operating model. For Kurnool, the district can play the role of local aggregator/quality assurance platform even without setting up a new corporation at the first stage.

The point for officials: replication must include guardrails. A registration and grading checklist prevents unsafe or low-quality centres from damaging the district brand.

Replication opportunity in Kurnool: cluster, do not scatter

Start where agriculture, tourism anchors and access already overlap.

Suggested pilot clusters

Minimum standard: 1-acre farm base; 5-acre threshold for school picnics can be adopted from Maharashtra as a safety benchmark.

Cluster pilots make administration, training, monitoring and marketing easier. Selection should consider access roads, toilets/water feasibility, crop calendar, farmer motivation, SHG participation, and proximity to existing tourist movement.

Decision slide: 90-day pilot launch pathway

A low-risk pilot can demonstrate demand before scale-up investments.

Constitute District AgriTourism Cell

Tourism, Agriculture, Horticulture, Education, DRDA/SERB, FPOs, SHGs

Select 8–12 pilot farms

Access, safety, horticulture diversity, toilets/water, motivated farmers

Training + product design

Hygiene, pricing, farm interpretation, school modules, safety SOP

Soft launch

Schools, officials, local influencers, tour operators, feedback capture

District rollout plan

Branding, booking page, convergence funds, monitoring dashboard

Immediate ask from District Administration

Approve pilot cell, shortlist farms, nominate nodal officers and permit a joint field visit to Maharashtra/ATDC for operational learning.

Close with a concrete action request. The pilot should be measured by visitors, farmer revenue, visitor satisfaction, hygiene/safety compliance, SHG/youth employment and repeat bookings.
