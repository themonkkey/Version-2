Baseline & endline

The same officers sit a knowledge paper before training and again after it. The baseline establishes what is already understood about Swarna Andhra, the KPI framework and the national-accounts concepts; the endline re-asks it, so the change is attributable to the session rather than to the cohort.

Design

Three levels, two sittings each

The paper is pitched to the level: district officers get the policy and KPI framing, constituency and mandal officers get the levers they actually control, master trainers get the estimation methodology they will teach on.

AP & Swarna Andhra — General Awareness

  • Vision AP 2030
  • Swarna Andhra @2047
  • Sunrise Andhra Plan
  • Nava Andhra Mission 2050
2Andhra Pradesh's Swarna Andhra @2047 vision aims to transform the state into a ₹308 lakh crore economy. What is this approximately in USD?
  • USD 500 billion
  • USD 1.2 trillion
  • USD 2.4 trillion
  • USD 4 trillion
3The Swarna Andhra @2047 vision targets what per capita income for AP citizens by 2047 (currently ~₹2.66 lakh)?
  • ₹15 lakh (~$12,000)
  • ₹30 lakh (~$23,000)
  • ₹55 lakh (~$42,000)
  • ₹75 lakh (~$57,000)
4Which of the following is NOT one of the core pillars of the Swarna Andhra @2047 Vision?
  • Zero Poverty and Inclusive Growth
  • Valley of Innovation and Speed of Business
  • Centralised Public Sector-led Industrial Growth
  • Tech-enabled Universal Healthcare
Section B · multiple choice

KPI Framework & Monitoring

5The Swarna Andhra @2047 KPI Portal monitors performance at which administrative levels?
  • State level only
  • District and state levels only
  • Mandal, district, constituency and state levels
  • Mandal and district levels only — state is monitored separately
6The KPI framework under Swarna Andhra @2047 is primarily aligned with which global framework?
  • BRICS Sustainable Growth Indicators
  • UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
  • World Bank Human Capital Index
  • NITI Aayog's Aspirational Districts Programme
7What annual growth rate does the Swarna Andhra @2047 vision document target for the AP economy?
  • 8% — in line with India's national average
  • 10% — matching the fastest-growing large states
  • 12% — as recommended by NITI Aayog
  • 15% — to reach USD 2.4 trillion by 2047
8As a district officer, what is your PRIMARY role in the Swarna Andhra KPI system?
  • Entering mandal-level data and forwarding it to the state without review
  • Entering, verifying and approving district/mandal level KPI achievement data
  • Setting district KPI targets independently based on local priorities
  • Reviewing KPI data only when a state-level audit is initiated
Section C · multiple choice

Economic Concepts — Quick Check

9What is the correct relationship between GVA and GDP?
  • GDP = GVA + Total Government Expenditure
  • GDP = GVA + Net Taxes on Products (Taxes minus Subsidies)
  • GDP = GVA adjusted for inflation using the Price Deflator
  • GDP = GVA minus Depreciation of fixed assets
10Which approach measures GDP by summing value added at each stage of production across all sectors?
  • Expenditure Approach — adds up all spending in the economy
  • Income Approach — sums wages, profits and rents
  • Production (Output) Approach — sums GVA across all sectors
  • Base Year Approach — fixes prices at a reference year
11The 'bottom-up approach' to estimating District Domestic Product means:
  • National GDP is calculated first and then disaggregated to districts using population or area shares
  • Economic data is collected and aggregated upward from district and mandal level
  • Only the lowest-income sectors in a district are measured to set a baseline
  • District-level GVA is estimated using state-level averages applied uniformly
12Which of the following economic activities belongs to the TERTIARY sector?
  • Aquaculture and prawn farming
  • Food processing in a registered factory
  • Wholesale and retail trade
  • Electricity generation from a thermal power plant
